5. Strategic Recommendations Based on Power BI Inspection
1. Capitalize on WhatsApp Broadcasts: 47.8% of teachers get course info from direct links and 26.9% from WhatsApp.
Stop relying on passive web portals; push 1-click links directly to WhatsApp groups.
2. Micro-Video Architecture: Since 80% learn at home after school and prefer 2-hour windows, break courses into
15-minute animated modules.
3. Formalize the CLSS Cascade: 28.3% of teachers share zero CLSS learnings upon return. Institute a 10-minute
mandatory Monday assembly debrief slot.
