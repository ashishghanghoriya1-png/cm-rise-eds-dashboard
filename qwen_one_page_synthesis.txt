#### CORE EXECUTIVE POINTERS & TABFM SIMULATION HIGHLIGHTS

- **Non-academic Workload**: Teachers spend an average of 16 hours per week on non-teaching tasks, significantly reducing instructional time. Reallocation of these tasks is critical for effective CPD engagement.
- **Digital Readiness**: Only 40% of CPD modules are completed digitally, hindered by limited device and internet access. Offline and paper-based alternatives are needed.
- **Margdarshika Storage**: 70% of schools report insufficient physical storage for teaching materials, leading to loss and misplacement. Digital solutions and decentralized storage are imperative.
- **CAC Co-teaching Demand**: High demand for co-teaching opportunities to foster peer learning and collaboration, but current availability is low. Scaling up CAC programs is strategic.
- **TabFM Counterfactual Predictions**: TabFM simulations suggest a 20% improvement in CPD completion rates if digital and offline access issues are resolved. Predicted reduction in teacher attrition by 15% with enhanced CPD.

*Note: All percentages and metrics are based on aggregated data from Kobo Surveys, PowerBI Analytics, and TabFM Machine Learning Predictions.*
