=== TABLE 1 ===
Ecosystem Diagnostic Study (EDS) | EDS is a structured diagnostic exercise that examines a teacher professional development (TPD) programme from the perspective of the teachers moving through it, to inform design decisions for the next planning cycle. It is designed in phased manner to cover all relevant stakeholders in the TPD programme.
Classroom Transfer | Classroom transfer, as EDS defines and codes it: evidence that a teacher actually put something learned from an intervention (IPT, CLSS, a digital course) into practice in their own classroom. These are confirmed on account of teachers’ self reporting.
CLSS learning cascade | Process in which participating teachers in monthly shaikshik samwad at the cluster level are expected to relay the learning to the other teachers in the school who couldn’t participate for the continued learning.
Structural Barriers | Refers to a reason a teacher couldn't attend particular intervention that sits entirely outside their own choice, motivation, or awareness- a physical, institutional, or circumstantial condition that makes attendance genuinely impossible, not just less likely.

=== TABLE 27 ===
Stated as a design principle: a TPD system that measures participation alone will systematically overstate its own impact, because participation is the input the system can observe, while transfer is the outcome it currently cannot. Closing this gap depends less on changing teacher behaviour than on building the system's capacity to observe what happens after delivery.

=== TABLE 29 ===
A study that hears only one side of a delivery system can identify every gap in it and still not establish where each gap originates. Pairing the teacher perspective with the officials' perspective is what turns a description of gaps into a diagnosis of their source, which is the information required for design decisions.

Element | Proposal
Respondents | DIET and BAC/CAC officials, Jan Shikshaks, CLSS facilitators and Master Trainers, district and block programme staff, and the mid-level academic officials currently being trained for CRO
Focus | How officials understand the purpose of each intervention and their own role within it; where they observe the system breaking down; the constraints under which they operate; and their perception of teacher engagement .
Method | In-depth interviews following this phase's paired-observation design, supplemented by a small number of focus group discussions at block and district level. . Integration | Findings paired directly with the teacher findings from this phase, intervention by intervention, to identify where each gap originates in the delivery chain
